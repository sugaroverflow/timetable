# Digest v2: per-forum, forum-branded, daily/weekly, full-text comments

**Date:** 2026-07-29

Ed's revision of the first draft:

- **Per-forum**: `computeUserForumDigests` returns one digest per forum
  with news — a member of three forums gets up to three emails. Sections
  are computed across memberships in the same queries as before, then
  grouped per forum.
- **Forum-branded**: the shell wordmark and the subject carry the FORUM
  name — subject `[Forum] Digest — 2 comments, 1 reply` — and links wear
  the forum theme's primary colour. The footer links that forum's
  Notifications page.
- **Daily/weekly**: `digestFrequency` + `digestWeekday` on
  NotificationSettings (account-level), a How-often selector in the
  member's digest card. The daily cron skips weekly users except on
  their chosen (UTC) day; skipping leaves `lastDigestAt` untouched so
  their window accumulates. First-run lookback is 7 days for weekly.
- **Full-text comments first**: comments on the recipient's topics lead
  the email as quoted full-text blocks; replies also full text.
  ❤️ stays a count.
- **Drafts**: "Your unpublished drafts" lists the recipient's own
  unpublished topics — a standing reminder that deliberately never
  triggers an email on its own (isForumDigestEmpty ignores it).
- **Cut**: the "Since your last digest: …" intro (the summary lives in
  the subject).
