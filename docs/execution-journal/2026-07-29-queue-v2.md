# Topic Queue v2: fresh-eyes cutoff, member-wide queue, ❤️ switcher + Next

**Date:** 2026-07-29

Ed's three-part design revision.

1. **"Hearts count from" resets the queue.** The cutoff now also resets
   review state: seen marks AND hearts from before it stop counting as
   "reviewed", so setting it (e.g. at the start of a term) refills every
   member's queue with all published topics — including currently-❤️'d
   ones, which come back to be re-affirmed or un-❤️'d. Mechanically the
   effective round start is `max(member's restart, forum cutoff)`, and
   the never-seen badge/Analysis Queue column count coverage since the
   cutoff. The queue is no longer "published minus hearted": a round is
   ALL published topics, and "reviewed" means seen-or-hearted since the
   relevant watermark (hearts union in because pre-queue hearts have no
   seen row).

2. **Every member gets the queue** (hosts asked): gate moved from
   `canHeart` to new shared `canUseQueue` (any member role). The sidebar
   badge stays red for electors — the reading is their vote — and is
   grey (`nav-badge-quiet`) for everyone else, who don't get in trouble
   for not doing the reading.

3. **Controls are now a ❤️ switcher + Next.** The switcher shows the
   topic's current heart state (🤍/❤️, `role="switch"`), saves on toggle
   but deliberately does NOT advance or refresh — hearting marks the
   topic seen server-side, so a refresh would skip it out from under the
   member. Next records seen and advances. Non-electors get Next alone.
   Same big round layout as v1's two buttons.

Consequences: the "You've ❤️'d every topic" end state is gone (hearted
topics are part of the round now); round restart brings back everything;
the cutoff form's copy explains the fresh-eyes reset.
